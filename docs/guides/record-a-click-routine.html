Record a click routine and replay it

Instead of placing points one by one, click through the routine once while the app watches. Every click becomes a step, with the wait you took before it.

Record it

  1. Under Target, choose Sequence.
  2. Press Record. The status bar says it is recording.
  3. Click through the routine at a normal pace: claim, close, next. Clicks on the autoclicker itself are ignored.
  4. Press Record again, or your Stop key (F7 by default), to finish.

Each click becomes a step with the mouse button you used, and the time since the previous click becomes that step's wait. Two quick clicks on the same spot become a double click. If the sequence already has steps, you choose whether to replace them, add the recording after them, or discard it.

Recording only notes where you click and when, never keystrokes. The mouse hook that sees the clicks exists only while recording and is removed as soon as you finish.

Tidy it up

  • Select a step and use Wait... to change how long to wait before the next one.
  • Up, Down and Remove rearrange steps; Add point picks one more by hand.
  • A sequence holds up to 50 steps.

Replay it

  1. Set Repeat to the number of rounds (0 keeps going until you stop it). The main Interval is the wait between rounds.
  2. Press Start (it counts down 3 seconds) or your Start key (F6, immediately).
  3. Stop any time with F7, Esc, or by pushing the mouse into a screen corner.

To keep it, press Save Profile: the steps and all the click settings are saved under a name, and you can run it later from a shortcut with WindowsAutoclicker.exe --profile "Daily" --start.
